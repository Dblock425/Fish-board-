What's biting in September

season sense

Read this one. You've been rigging for halibut, but halibut peaks January to May and is well off its window. Late September still belongs to the basses. Lead with spotted bay bass in the harbor, at peak through October, and treat a halibut as the bonus. Rockfish and cabezon open October 1, this Thursday, so early October rewrites this whole board. Water is holding near 73.5°, which is why bonito and mackerel are thick on the piers.

Before you keep anything: halibut 22 in minimum, saltwater basses 14 in with a 5-fish combined limit. Size and bag limits change year to year, so measure every fish and confirm current rules on the CDFW site before you put one in the bag.
